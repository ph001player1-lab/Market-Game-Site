WHY WE EXIST

Doing business on an island is easier together

Many of us came to Phuket from somewhere else — others grew up here and watched the island change. Everyone learns local business from their own mistakes. We would rather learn it together.

Mission

Bring Phuket’s entrepreneurs together, so nobody has to figure out business on the island alone.

Goal

A community where owners know each other by name, share what works and send business to each other.

How we get there

We meet 2–6 times a month. The game is one of the formats — alongside workshops, talks, breakfasts and evenings.

THE MODEL

How chambers of commerce work in the US — on Phuket

In American towns, local business owners have gathered in chambers of commerce for generations: they meet regularly, know each other by name, recommend each other and learn from each other. The formula is simple and works anywhere — including on an island.

  1. Meet often

    Not once a year at a conference, but several times a month — until faces turn into names.

  2. Help first

    Advice, a contact, a recommendation to a client. Members give before they ask.

  3. Learn from practice

    Workshops and talks by people who run businesses here, on the problems of this season.

  4. Do business with each other

    Suppliers, partners, clients: the first place to look is the community.

THE BUSINESS GAME

“Capture the Market” — two hours of real competition

Several teams run competing businesses on one market and fight for the same customers. Every turn is a game month: price, quality, advertising, staff, loans. Where customers go is decided by the model, not by the host.

After the game we go through the decisions together, and everyone at the table says what they would do differently. That is where people really get to know each other.

QUESTIONS

Before your first meeting

Do I need to be a member to come?

No. Every meeting is open: pick one in the schedule, sign up and come.

Can I come alone?

Yes, that is how most people come. At games, teams are formed right at the table.

Do I pay in advance?

No. Games are paid per team at the meeting — $25 per team. You can also come just to watch.

Where are meetings held?

At hotels, cafés and coworkings on the island. We send the address personally after you sign up.

What if too few people sign up?

A meeting needs at least four people. If fewer sign up, it may be cancelled — we will warn you in advance and suggest another date.

What language are meetings in?

Mostly English. If you are more comfortable in Russian or Thai, mention it when you sign up.

What should I bring?

A phone for the game, business cards if you have them, and a question you would like to ask other owners.

My business isn’t a restaurant — is the game for me?

Yes. The restaurant in the game is only a shell: price, quality, advertising, capacity and competitors work the same way for villas, tours and agencies.
